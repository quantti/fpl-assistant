# Penalty and Set-Piece Takers

Reference document on dead-ball duties for all 20 Premier League clubs for 2026/27: penalty takers with backups, direct free-kick takers, and corner/indirect free-kick takers. Compiled 16 August 2026 from the FFScout set-piece hub and penalty-taker article, the r/FantasyPL community table "Set-piece takers for GW1 (version 0)" and its correction comments, cross-checked against the official player list. Duties change with injuries, signings and form — always verify against current team news before advising.

## How to read this

- **Penalties** are listed in order: first choice, deputy, third in line. Clubs marked "nailed" have a single established taker who took the large majority of spot-kicks last season.
- **Direct free kicks** and **corners/indirect free kicks** are listed in order of prominence.
- A player who is a set-piece taker but **not in the official FPL player list** is flagged — they cannot be selected, which matters when the duty matters for FPL.
- Some entries carried over from last season are stale; the community corrections that fix them are noted per club.

## Where the duties matter for FPL

- A nailed penalty taker at a good price is a structural boost: Haaland, Bruno Fernandes, Palmer, Igor Thiago, Szoboszlai, Groß (£5.5m), Ndiaye (£6.0m) and Calvert-Lewin (£6.0m) all carry spot-kick upside on top of their attacking profile.
- Set-piece roles also settle close calls: Groß's penalties and corners make him the community's preferred Brighton mid; Szoboszlai's corners, free kicks and likely pens explain his 42% ownership at £7.0m; Harry Wilson's free-kick share is part of the Leeds interest at £6.5m.
- The clubs with the least clarity (Fulham, Bournemouth, Ipswich, Hull) are where a single pre-season friendly can move a pick's value — check the latest news before relying on a pen claim there.
