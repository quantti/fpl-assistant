# FPL Promoted Clubs and New Signings

Reference document with two separate evaluation tasks: how to assess newly promoted clubs' FPL assets, and how to assess new signings. They are deliberately different problems — a promoted team's players and a star arrival at a top club are not the same kind of bet — so each has its own framework. Prices and ownership are the official FPL figures as of 16 August 2026.

## Evaluating promoted clubs (2026/27: Coventry City, Hull City, Ipswich Town)

- **Championship numbers do not translate.** Burnley went from 30 second-tier clean sheets in 2024/25 to four in the Premier League. A promoted side's Championship xGC and shut-out totals are a starting hint, not a projection — the community's rule is that defensive stats rarely carry up.
- **What matters instead**: (a) whether the team defends in a way that generates defensive contribution (DefCon) points — a direct, deep block produces clearances and blocks regardless of results; (b) set-piece organisation (promoted teams live and die by set pieces); (c) price and minutes security; (d) whether the club spent to stay up.
- **The £4.0–4.5m bracket is where promoted value lives.** Coventry: Thomas (£4.0m), van Ewijk (£4.0m, most-owned of the group at 15.3%). Hull: Hughes, Egan, Mendy, Herrington (all £4.0m — the deepest pool, but Hull's defence was bad even in the Championship, per the community). Ipswich: Diop (£4.0m, 18.4% owned, an established Premier League centre-back with a strong DefCon profile) and O'Shea (£4.0m). For these picks, chase the DefCon floor, not clean sheets — and remember the price-drop risk that comes with very high ownership (price moves with buy/sell volume).
- **Goalkeepers**: Rushworth (Coventry £4.5m) is fine only inside a rotation; Butland (Hull £4.5m) and Scherpen (Ipswich £4.5m) are the promoted starters.
- **Attacking options are cheap but unproven**: Coventry — Wright (£5.5m FWD), Torp (£5.5m MID), plus Awoniyi (a £5.5m FWD whose move to Coventry was agreed in pre-season); Ipswich — Hirst (£5.0m FWD), Clarke and Philogene (£5.5m MIDs); Hull — McBurnie (£5.5m FWD), Crooks (£4.5m MID), Belloumi (£5.0m MID). They are watchlist players, not GW1 starters.
- **Fixtures decide timing.** All three promoted clubs open against last season's top sides (Arsenal, Man Utd, Liverpool and Man City among the first four Gameweeks), so their assets are bench material at GW1. The buying window comes when their fixture runs turn — e.g. Fulham face all three promoted clubs in GW6–8, and the promoted clubs' own easy runs arrive later in the season.

## Evaluating new signings

- **Seven-question framework** for any new arrival:
  1. **Role and position** — check the official classification: several 2026/27 signings were reclassified (Caicedo now a midfielder, O'Reilly and Matheus N. now defenders), which changes everything about their value.
  2. **Minutes security** — nailed starter or rotation risk? A signing is not a starter by default.
  3. **Settling-in risk** — players new to the league (Kroupi at Bournemouth, Estêvão at Chelsea, Woltemade at Newcastle, Reijnders and Cherki at Man City) need time; "new to the league" is a reason to watch, not to own.
  4. **Known quantity versus projection** — proven at this level (Groß back at Brighton, Xhaka at Sunderland, Calvert-Lewin at Leeds, Brobbey at Sunderland, Kelleher at Brentford) is a different bet from a projection.
  5. **Price-point versus peers** — is the price a discount (reclassification, new club) or a premium (star tax)?
  6. **Set-piece and penalty duty** — check the penalty and set-piece takers document before assuming a signing inherits dead-ball duty.
  7. **Pre-season evidence** — minutes in the last friendlies matter; friendly goals do not.
- **2026/27's big moves in two buckets.** The premium signings priced as stars and expected to start immediately: Isak and Wirtz (Liverpool), Semenyo, Guéhi, Donnarumma and Elliot Anderson (Man City), Mbeumo, Cunha and Sesko (Man Utd), João Pedro and Rogers (Chelsea), Gyökeres and Tzolis (Arsenal), Lacroix (Chelsea). The value signings priced to their new club, where role is the open question: Brobbey, E.Le Fée and Xhaka (Sunderland), Calvert-Lewin and Nmecha (Leeds), Groß and Gomez (Brighton), Wissa (Newcastle), Kelleher (Brentford), Van Hecke and Senesi (Spurs).
- **New managers double the unknown.** Several 2026/27 arrivals come with new coaches (Carrick at Man Utd, Maresca at Man City, De Zerbi at Spurs, Iraola at Liverpool, Glasner at Forest, Jaissle at Newcastle, Alonso at Chelsea). A signing under a new manager is two unknowns — role and system — so treat pre-season line-ups as the evidence, not the reputation.
- **The community's rule**: watch rather than own at GW1 unless the price makes the pick disposable. Late-returning World Cup players (summer 2026) are the minutes risk to monitor before trusting a new signing's pre-season minutes.
