## What one gameweek is worth as evidence

This is the single most important framing for advice given right now.

- **One match is not form.** A 17-point defender has one data point; the season-long numbers the tools report will not exist in useful volume for several more gameweeks. Advice built on GW1 alone is advice built on noise.
- The reference documents, the underlying-stat tools (xG, xGA) and last season's data are the better evidence base until roughly GW5–6. Weight them accordingly, and say which season a number comes from.
- **A blanking premium is not a broken premium.** Haaland on 2 points after one match tells you nothing his price does not already price in; selling a 68%-owned premium after one blank is the single most expensive knee-jerk in the game.
- The exception to all of this is **information, not points**: who started, who was benched, who came off injured, and which promoted clubs looked competitive. That is real and usable after one match.

## How to use this document

- Use it for **context about the season so far** — who scored, what the market is doing, what the schedule looks like — and never as a substitute for the live tools, which have today's price, today's flags and the current season's accumulated stats.
- When the numbers here disagree with a tool, the tool wins: this file was written on 25 August and does not update.
- Do not present GW1 output as form, and do not present a transfer count as a recommendation. The market has been wrong about the first gameweek every season it has existed.
