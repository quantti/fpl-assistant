# 2026/27 Pre-Season: Template, Value Picks and Community Consensus

Timely document capturing the 2026/27 Gameweek 1 consensus as of 16 August 2026: the template, value picks, early fixtures and live community debates. Prices and ownership are official FPL figures at that date and move every day until the GW1 deadline (Friday 21 August, 17:30 UTC). Cross-check anything cited here with live stats tools before advising.

## Season context: what changed for 2026/27

- **Scoring change — defensive contribution points ("DefCon")**: a new scoring category was introduced and the BPS system was rebalanced around it. Centre-backs lose a large share of bonus points compared to last season; midfielders, full-backs and goalkeepers gain. Bonus points now appear live from the 20th minute, and points, ranks and mini-leagues update live. The community reads this as: cheap centre-backs with high DefCon output are the value play, and premium defensive spending needs more scrutiny.
- **Post-World Cup summer**: the 2026 World Cup pushed key players' returns late; minutes uncertainty for tournament finalists is a recurring theme in community drafts.
- **Promoted**: Coventry City, Hull City, Ipswich Town. **Relegated**: Burnley, West Ham, Wolves. The promoted clubs' cheap defenders (all £4.0–4.5m) are the main rookie interest — as bench fodder, not GW1 starters, because their opening fixtures are brutal.
- **A huge transfer market** (all confirmed in the official player list): Isak → Liverpool (£9.0m FWD), Wirtz → Liverpool (£7.5m MID), Semenyo → Man City (£8.5m MID), Guéhi → Man City (£6.0m DEF), Donnarumma → Man City (£5.5m GKP), Elliot Anderson → Man City (£6.5m MID), Cherki → Man City (£7.5m MID), Mbeumo → Man Utd (£8.0m MID), Cunha → Man Utd (£8.0m MID), João Pedro → Chelsea (£7.5m FWD), Morgan Rogers → Chelsea (£7.5m MID), Lacroix → Chelsea (£6.0m DEF), Calvert-Lewin → Leeds (£6.0m FWD), Brobbey → Sunderland (£6.0m FWD), E.Le Fée → Sunderland (£6.0m MID), Xhaka → Sunderland (£5.5m MID), Groß → Brighton (£5.5m MID), Van Hecke → Spurs (£5.0m DEF), Senesi → Spurs (£6.0m DEF), Tzolis → Arsenal (£6.5m MID), Gyökeres → Arsenal (£7.5m FWD), Wissa → Newcastle (£6.0m FWD), Madueke → Arsenal (£6.5m MID).
- **Post-Salah / post-TAA Liverpool**: Salah and Trent Alexander-Arnold have left; the premium-midfield mantle passes to Bruno Fernandes. Several big clubs changed managers (Chelsea under Alonso, Newcastle under Matthias Jaissle) and Spurs are mid-rebuild — the community calls this "the most uncertainty in 11 years of playing FPL".

## The GW1 template

- **Premiums**: Haaland (MCI £15.5m FWD, ~73% owned) is the near-mandatory anchor and captaincy favourite (BOU, CRY, COV in GW1–3). Bruno Fernandes (MUN £12.0m MID, ~48%) is the premium-mid anchor — 235 points last season, penalties, set pieces, and Hull (A) + Ipswich (H) to open. Saka (ARS £9.5m, ~11%) and Palmer (CHE £9.5m, ~11%) split the second-premium-mid votes; Isak (LIV £9.0m, ~14%) is the popular second forward.
- **The consensus core** (agreed across sources): Haaland + Bruno + João Pedro + Szoboszlai + Raya + Gabriel. OneFPL calls this the "strong template"; FPL Pilot's projection-led draft is Verbruggen/Dubravka; N.Williams/Shaw/Kayode/Hume/Van Hecke; Szoboszlai/B.Fernandes/Ndiaye/Garner/Xhaka; Haaland/Thiago/Calvert-Lewin (£98.0m spent, £2.0m in the bank, GW1–5 projections).
- **Captaincy**: Haaland dominates (56% of projected top-1k votes per Onside), then Saka (~18%) and Bruno (~12%). A captain pick outside the top three is a clear differential bet; riding the template captain means blanking with the field.
- **Ownership snapshot (16 Aug)**: Haaland 73.3%, João Pedro 57.3%, Bruno 47.8%, Szoboszlai 41.9%, Raya 31.4%, Semenyo 28.5%, Mbeumo 27.9%, Gabriel 27.5%, Rogers 26.5%, Calvert-Lewin 25.7%, Shaw 23.8%, Pedro Porro 23.2%, Guéhi 22.3%, Dubravka 22.3%, O'Reilly 22.1%, Rice 21.3%, Kinsky 19.9%, Lammens 19.1%, Calafiori 18.5%, Verbruggen 18.5%, Diop 18.4%, Virgil 17.1%, Thiago 16.4%, Ndiaye 15.8%, Brobbey 15.8%, Konsa 15.7%, van Ewijk 15.3%, Wirtz 15.0%, Isak 14.1%, Gvardiol 13.9%, Watkins 12.4%, Groß 11.5%.
- **How much template**: sources recommend 6–8 high-ownership players and 65–80% template overlap plus one or two sub-10% differentials. The template protects rank; the differentials win leagues.

## Live community debates (16 Aug)

- **Bruno vs no-Bruno**: genuinely split. For: Hull/Ipswich to open, 235 points last season, penalties and set pieces, "easier to transfer him out than to bring him in without a wildcard". Against: £12.0m funds big upgrades ("the only massive upgrade I see is having Semenyo"), and Haaland has outscored Bruno in each of the last four seasons (272–176, 217–166, 181–174, 239–235 — twice close). The community leans start-with-Bruno, exit on the fixture swing.
- **Haaland hold or fold**: ~73% own. The no-Haaland side notes identical PPG to Bruno last season at £3.5m more; the default view is that he is the best player in the league and the captaincy anchor, and buying him later without a wildcard is harder than starting with him.
- **Man Utd defence**: Shaw (£4.5m, 23.8%) versus Maguire (£5.0m, 10.7%) versus "avoid our defence entirely". The first two fixtures are good but the back line is called "a complete shambles"; Maguire is widely framed as a two-week punt with an urgent transfer out, and "Shaw feels like a trap".
- **Mbeumo at Man Utd** (£8.0m, 27.9%): some are spooked by him not starting the latest friendly; others treat him as one of two nailed United attackers with amazing fixtures.
- **No-Arsenal drafts**: common among engaged managers. Gabriel is "excellent but expensive"; with Saliba absent, a cheaper £5.5m Arsenal defender is being watched. The Community Shield confirmed Tzolis over Martinelli on the wing.
- **Arsenal striker minutes**: Gyökeres (£7.5m, 12.8%) vs Havertz (£7.5m, 3.1%) — the Community Shield line-up put Havertz ahead for big games; Gyökeres is a rotation-risk pick.
- **Brighton triple-ups**: the informed crowd owns Groß, Verbruggen, Vuskovic/Boscagli, Gomez and Kostoulas; Vuskovic is "as nailed as it gets" as Van Hecke's replacement. Overall ownership lags the talk (Brighton ~12th in squad-slot share), so this is a consensus of engaged managers, not the whole player base.
- **The DefCon meta**: "central defenders are still the play." Best DefCon/90 from pre-season minutes (small samples, treat with caution): Ampadu (LEE) 16.7, Bogarde (AVL) 15.9, Gnonto (LEE) 15.3, Vuskovic (BHA) 13.1, Caicedo (CHE) 11.3, Kostoulas (BHA) 11.0, Mount (MUN) 10.9, Fofana (CHE) 10.6, Andrey Santos (MUN) 9.9, Ayari (BHA) 9.8, Boscagli (BHA) 9.1.
- **Bench Boost plans**: Bench Boost GW1 and GW2 drafts are circulating; several managers abandoned BB GW2 when a cheap enabler missed the last two friendlies. Early wildcard (GW6–8) is the common escape hatch, and "no booked transfers" is the repeated advice.
- **Pre-season form caveats**: João Pedro's 8 goals and Brentford's 7–0 over Frankfurt are generating hype, but the community's own warning applies — "every template popular person is slaying today which we know what that means in a week". Friendly output is the noisiest signal there is. Bruno missed two pre-season penalties and is still expected to keep the ball.
