# Bench Boost Playbook

Reference guide to the Bench Boost chip: when it pays, how to prepare, and the failure modes.

## When to bench boost

Bench Boost counts all 15 players' points for one Gameweek. The best week is a Double Gameweek where all 15 are expected to start, with favourable fixtures and low rotation risk.

## How to prepare

Build toward the target Gameweek over several transfers: upgrade dead bench spots, prioritise minutes over upside on the bench, and plan backward from the boost week rather than in the deadline week.

## Common mistakes

Using Bench Boost with a non-playing bench wastes the slot. A bench of four cheap non-starters adds nothing.
