The other two keep the original history FIFO; canonical preemption and ready/blocked/untouched fixture turns remain. The test now requires STAGED within 400 virtual seconds / 350 admissions, at most 70 MB returned raw body values and its independent oracle checks every required history's exact season/epoch, effective source floor and completion before the fixture completed.
