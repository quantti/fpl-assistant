## Task 1 — explain the bounded progress result (done)

`HistoryFixtureDispatcher.processFixture` requires both clubs' roster plus live/fixture-stat participant IDs, each with VALID same-epoch history fetched **after** the fixture response. `OwnedSyncStore.fixtureReadinessPredicate` enforces the manifest, requested demand and fixture-read floors. This coverage requirement protects against missing participants; do not remove roster members, relax time/epoch fences or treat an older response as fresh merely to stage a fixture. Fair claim selection rotates history and fixture classes but does not favour missing players across all manifests. The coordinator also enqueues the full roster. These mechanisms can distribute valid history completions across fixtures and pre-anchor reads.

The epoch-76 postflight found 84 completed histories, 15 persisted fixture manifests and zero fixtures STAGED. The best eligible overlap was 37/71 for one fixture; another had 48/68 histories without a source floor but only 33/68 at its floor. All 15 manifests were incomplete. This **explains why the aggregate 84 did not stage one of those fixtures**, not which mechanism dominates or whether an unlimited run would eventually converge. The other 35 fixture runs had no persisted manifest; do not assign them the same specific cause from aggregate evidence alone. Existing two-worker finite-drain tests use 68 roster histories and 3–4 players per club, unlike the observed 56–80 dependencies per fixture.
